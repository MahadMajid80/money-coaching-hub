# Editing your website

Log in at **moneycoachinghub.com/admin**. The welcome screen has shortcuts to the things you'll change most. Everything you save shows on the site straight away.

## The everyday jobs

**Change a course date or price**
Courses → open the course → *Booking* tab. Set **First session**: the homepage and course page dates follow it automatically. Change **Price** and **Payment link (Stripe)** in the same place. Press **Save**.

**Open or close a course**
In the course, change **Status** (right-hand side) to *Open for booking*, *Waiting list* or *Hidden*.

**Write a blog post**
Blog posts → **Create new**. Add a title, pick a **Category**, add a **Featured image**, then write in the *Article* box. Type `/` in the article box to add headings, lists, quotes or images.
- **Save draft** keeps it private. The eye icon shows it next to the site as you type.
- **Publish** puts it live. It appears on the homepage and blog straight away.
- To publish later, use **Schedule publish** next to the Publish button.
- The **SEO** fields at the bottom set the Google title and description. "Auto-generate" fills them for you.

**Edit or delete a post**
Open it from Blog posts. To delete, tick it in the list and press **Delete**, or use the ⋯ menu inside the post.

**Add your latest Times of Malta column**
Times of Malta columns → **Create new** → paste the title, link and date. The two newest show on the homepage.

**Add a review**
Reviews → **Create new**. Add the quote, name, job title and an optional photo, and tick where it should appear: Homepage, Coaching, Workshops or My story. Change **Order** to move it up or down.

**Change any text or photo on a page**
Pages → choose the page (Homepage, Coaching page, …) → open a section → edit → **Save**.
- Wrap words in `*stars*` to keep the gold highlight, and in `**double stars**` for bold.
- To use a different photo, click **Choose from existing** or drag one in. Remove it to go back to the original.
- **Hide this section** takes a whole section off the page without deleting it.
- Empty a box to go back to the original wording.

**Client logos ("Trusted by teams at")**
Client logos → open a client → upload the logo (white or transparent works best). Clients without a logo show as text.

## Less often

- **Menu, footer, phone, email, social links:** Settings → Site settings.
- **Announcement bar** (e.g. "January cohort: 5 seats left"): Site settings → *Header & menu* → Announcement bar.
- **Google Analytics / Meta Pixel:** Site settings → *Tracking*, paste the ID.
- **Side Hustle page stage** (Get notified → Intro → Programme) and its payment links: Side hustle: launch stage.
- **Quiz words:** Quiz words. Each word counts towards one money type.
- **Privacy, cookies, terms, or a new page:** Pages → Simple pages. A new page appears at `/its-slug/`.
- **An old link that should go somewhere new:** Settings → Redirects.
- **Messages from the site:** Inbox → Enquiries. Tick *Dealt with* when you've replied. Newsletter sign-ups are listed under Inbox too.
- **Undo a change:** open the page or post → **Versions** (top right) → pick an earlier version → Restore.
- **Give someone else access:** Settings → Users → Create new. *Editor* can change content. *Admin* can also manage logins.

## Photos

Upload in Media library or straight from any image box. After uploading, click the photo and set the **focus point** on the face, so crops never cut heads off. Fill in **Alt text** (a few words describing the photo): it helps Google and visitors using screen readers.
